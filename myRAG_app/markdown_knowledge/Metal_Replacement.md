Speaker notes:
A vat of liquid photopolymer resin is cured through selective exposure to light (via a laser of projector) which then initiates polymerization and converts the exposed areas to a solid part
Material is extruded through a nozzle or orifice in tracks or beads, which are then combined into multi-layer models. Common varieties include heated thermoplastic extrusion (similar to hot glue gun) and syringe dispensing.
