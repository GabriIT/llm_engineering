3. Details
Background
• Murakoshi has dimension instability issue for some applications using unreinforced PA 66. As
the countermeasure, FUJT and MIHI suggested PA612, Grilamid 2D 20. Mrakoshi accepted
this proposal and selected a first test application. They selected door latch parts and arranged
kick-off meeting with their moulder " Koshin Kasei". They requested explanation about

### Segment 2 (page 2)

2

difference between PA 66 and PA 612 as to properties. This time, Mrakoshi, Inabata and
EMS visited Koshin Kasei.
• However, at the start of this meeting, Murakoshi told us that a first test part was changed from
door latch parts to door guide tool under the closet.
Situation in usage of PA 66
• Tolerance of inner diameter (Metal shaft is inserted) : ±0.05 mm

• Pre-delivery inspection by Koshin Kasei is passed as dry condition. Parts are delivered without
conditioning.
• After receiving production parts, Murakoshi store them in their warehouse. No clear rule about
storage period.
• Acceptance inspection is done before assembly process in Murakoshi. Sometimes, Murakoshi
QA judges that dimension is out of tolerance because of swelling.
• Koshin Kasei is requesting improvement of this situation. Murakoshi decided to try less
moisture uptake material this time.
• Swelling by moisture uptake causes bigger gap between a door guide tool and a metal shaft.
• A metal shaft moves up and down. Then, bigger gap causes instable up-and-down motion.
Current material
• 1
st information from Mrakoshi was Asahi Leona 1300S (Standard unrei nforced PA 66).
However, current material used for the door guide was PA66 recycle material " TANAJIN🄬🄬
TN720 or TN710" which is PIR from fiber and film waste in production factory.
• TAKAYASU URL: https://www.takayasu-rf.co.jp/business/nylon.html

• 3 Plates, Pin gates -> Sub runner with submarine gates, 4 cavities
Injection moulding machine
• Sumitomo SE100EV-A https://www.koushinkasei.com/equi_guide.html
• Vent type barrel of Nihon Yuki is equipped.
• Nihon Yuki "Hungly feeder" is used.
